# Time Machine

A walkable 3D Ancient Rome, seen at street level, as it stood around 128 AD under Hadrian. Explore it like Street View, discover 20 landmarks and read the history behind each one.

Built with plain HTML/JS and [Three.js](https://threejs.org) (loaded from a CDN). No build step, no install.

## Play

Serve the folder and open it in a browser:

```bash
ruby -run -e httpd . -p 8123
# or: python3 -m http.server 8123
```

Then visit http://localhost:8123. Because it is static, it also works on GitHub Pages as-is.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Walk / strafe | Arrow keys or `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| Run | `Shift` |
| Look | Drag the mouse (double-click to lock the mouse) |
| Turn | `Q` `E` |
| Map (click to teleport) | `M` |
| List of places | `P` |
| Time of day | `T` |
| Fight (in the Colosseum arena) | `F` to start. `Space` or click attacks, hold `Shift` or right-click blocks, `WASD` moves, `Esc` leaves |
| Animal hunt (in the arena) | `U`. Bow and arrows: hold `Space` or the mouse to draw, release to shoot, `Shift` dodge roll, `WASD` move |
| Lion (in the arena) | `L`. A lion on its own, hunted with the bow (same controls as the animal hunt) |
| Naval battle (in the arena) | `N`. The arena floods and you command a galley. `W` `S` sail, `A` `D` steer, `Q` / `E` fire port / starboard cannons, `Space` or click fires every cannon on both sides at once (cannonballs carry about 15 m, so you have to get close) |
| Watch (Colosseum or Circus stands) | `V` to start or stop. In the Colosseum it shows the day's programme: `N` skips to the next act, `1`-`5` picks one. `C` switches to a cinematic camera, `WASD` lets you walk around the stands |
| Race (on the Circus Maximus track) | `R` to start. `W` gas, `S` brake, `A` `D` steer (hold `Shift` for fine steering), `Q` `E` hop a lane, `1`-`9` speed level (1 slowest, 9 gallop), `Space` whip, `X` cruise control, `C` cycle camera, hold `B` to look back, `Esc` leaves |

## What you can do

- Walk the Via Sacra through the Forum, up the steps of the temples, and into the Colosseum (climb the seats) and the Pantheon
- Discover landmarks by walking near them. Progress is saved in your browser
- Jump anywhere from the map or the Places list
- **Old Delhi:** press `B` (or the ✈ Travel button) and choose Old Delhi to travel to Shahjahanabad, Shah Jahan's walled capital, and back to Rome. Walk Chandni Chowk (a canal down the middle, the moonlit pool, shops and bazaars on both sides) from the Fatehpuri Masjid to the Lahori Gate of the Red Fort, then through Chhatta Chowk and the Naubat Khana to the Diwan-i-Aam, the marble Diwan-i-Khas and Rang Mahal beside the Stream of Paradise, and the Muthamman Burj over the river wall. South of the avenue the Jama Masjid stands on its rock with flights of steps up to the courtyard, and you have a plot of your own off Chandni Chowk: you start with a small one-room house, and the Market sells a house with a garden (150), a grand haveli (400, rent 15 a morning) and a mahal with a tower (1,000, rent 40), each healing you faster at home. Rome and Delhi each keep their own house. `K` switches between **Mughal Delhi, c. 1650** (elephants and bullock carts on the avenue, the canal, Begum ki Serai, the fort's gardens) and **Modern Delhi** (the Town Hall, cycle rickshaws, the Chandni Chowk Metro entrance, barracks in the fort, the flag over the Lahori Gate). 11 sights to discover (14 in Modern Delhi: the Metro station, **India Gate** and **Kartavya Path**). A long road runs south from the Jama Masjid lane to New Delhi: in Modern Delhi it ends at India Gate (a 42 m sandstone arch with the names carved on its walls, three granite steps, the canopy with the statue behind it, and the ceremonial avenue between its two canals and lawns); in Shah Jahan's day the same ground is open scrubland. **Games of Old Delhi (`N` in Delhi):** a race down Chandni Chowk (you choose your vehicle first: a rickshaw, Vespa scooter, city car or sports car in Modern Delhi, an elephant or a bullock cart in Mughal Delhi; the three rivals ride the same kind; faster vehicles go faster but are harder to steer through the traffic, with a boost, nine speed levels on keys `1`-`9` and cruise on `X`: the higher the level, the faster you tire, so pace yourself), *Defend the Red Fort* (aim a cannon on the Lahori Gate at three waves of raiders), *kushti* wrestling in a mud pit (take hold, then mash Space to throw; first to two throws), *kite fights* (fly your kite across the others' strings and hold Space to saw through them), a *chaat run* (carry hot food to three customers through the crowds before it cools or spills), *pachisi* (the Mughal board game of cowrie shells, against a rival) and a *bazaar trader* game (buy cheap in one bazaar and sell dear in another over six days; the bazaars open at 6 am and close at 7 pm, each trade takes 15 minutes and a walk to another bazaar 2 hours, and the clock shows the time of day). Each game has its own place in the city (the akhara, the race arches, a cannon on the fort wall, a kite stall, a pachisi pavilion, a bazaar stall and a chaat stall): walk up and press `F`, or find them in Places and Directions (`G`), where they appear after the sights. All single-player for now. The fights, races and naval battles stay in Rome. The places and facts come from the history of the sites; building shapes are simplified and not to scale between districts
- **Exploring pays:** every new sight you discover, in Rome or Delhi, pays 50 denarii (once per sight; your house and the game spots do not count).
- **Other players:** when the game server is reachable, other people walking around the same city appear near you as named characters (`👥 n other players nearby` under your stats). They are only for show: you can't touch or hurt each other outside the online games. They also see your clothes and your companion: a hound trotting behind you or an eagle circling above. Your vehicle too: they see you riding a car, scooter, rickshaw, cart or elephant, and they see the one you left parked. You only see people within about 260 m, up to 30 at a time, in the same city (Rome or Delhi).
- **Weather and seasons:** a year is 28 days (seven per season: spring, summer, autumn and winter, which Delhi calls spring, summer, monsoon and winter). The weather changes every few game hours: clear, cloudy, rain, thunderstorms (flashes and thunder) or fog that closes in around you. The odds depend on the season and the city: Rome has damp winters and dry summers, Delhi has a monsoon and thick winter fog. The sky greys, rain falls and hisses, and the grass and leaves change colour with the season. A badge next to the era shows the weather and season. It is worked out from the day and hour, so it needs no server.
- **Chat:** press `Enter` (or the 💬 Chat button) to talk to everyone who is online, `Enter` again to send, `Esc` to close. `/w name message` whispers to one person (names with spaces work), `/help` lists the commands. The server cleans messages, limits how fast you can send them and masks swearing; new people see the last 40 messages. **Admin:** accounts listed in the `admins` table (see `supabase-admin.sql`) get a 🛠 Admin button with the list of who has signed up (email, character, when they joined and last logged in) and who is online, with Mute and Kick buttons, plus the chat commands `/who`, `/kick name`, `/mute name [minutes]`, `/unmute name` and `/say message`. **Forgot your password?** There is a button on the log-in screen that emails a reset link (set the Site URL in Supabase, Authentication > URL Configuration).
- **Weather and seasons (`weather.js`):** clear, partly cloudy, cloudy, windy (blowing leaves, a rushing sound), drizzle, rain, thunderstorm, fog, mist, snow (the ground and trees turn white), and severe events: hail (it stings), blizzards (Rome's winter), tornadoes (a funnel that wanders the land, drags you in and kills you if you stay) and cyclones (their winds shove you along; a Delhi monsoon cyclone, a Mediterranean hurricane in Rome's autumn). The weather changes every day: each day has its own theme (never the same as yesterday's), and severe days (hailstorms, blizzards in winter, tornadoes, and cyclones or hurricanes in the monsoon and autumn) come about once a week, with a build-up before and a calm after. Rome and Delhi share the same seasons (spring, summer, autumn, winter; autumn is Delhi's monsoon) but have different weather, and never the same sort of weather at the same moment. Clouds drift across the sky: a few in partly cloudy weather, many when it is cloudy, and a full grey ceiling in storms. The badge at the top shows it.
- **The market (`stalls.js`):** there is no Market button. A market square stands south of the Forum in Rome and south of the Lahori Gate in Old Delhi (in Places as *The Market*), with five stalls: food, clothier, animals and rides, builder, healer. Walk up and press `F`.
- **Hunger:** a Food bar runs down from full to empty in about 12 minutes. Eat at the food stall (each dish fills it and heals you; the menu depends on city and era, with masala dosa in Delhi). Under 20% you lose health and stop healing; you can starve.
- **Dresser (`dresser.js`):** every house has one (press `F` in front): see what you own to wear and put it on or take it off. Nothing is sold there.
- **Garages (`garage.js`):** every upgraded house (not the small starting one) has a garage in Rome and Old Delhi. Press `F` at its open front to take a vehicle out or park it, or click your parked vehicle with the mouse to send it to the garage.
- **Jobs at the Colosseum (`jobs.js`):** a stair-house by the west gate leads to the hypogeum. Take 40-second shifts: winch crew (`A`/`D`), beast keeper (`1`-`4`), scenery crew (`Space`). Pay is in denarii times your rank (Hand, Journeyman, Foreman, Master of the Hypogeum).
- **Shipyard (`shipyard.js`):** press `N` in the arena. Every boat is a different size and holds a different number of cannons: kayak 1, canoe 2, fishing boat 4, liburna 6, galley 8, trireme 10, quinquereme 12, battleship 14, each with its own cannon positions. Click an empty circle to put a cannon there; click a cannon to choose which way it points (ahead, ahead and out, straight out of the side, astern and out, or astern) or to take it off. Each saved ship is shown in the list with a small picture of where its cannons are. Choose a ship type (Kayak, Canoe, Fishing boat, Liburna, Galley, Trireme, Quinquereme or Battleship: from a 30-hull kayak with 2 cannons to a 260-hull battleship with 14), put cannons on any of seven positions along each side, pick colours and a name, save up to four ships (browser and account) and sail one into the naval battle. More cannons on a side hit harder but reload slower. In *Play online > Naval battle* you pick one of your saved ships from a list (you cannot build one there); the server checks the design (type, number of cannons) and uses its hull, speed and cannons, and the other captains see your ship. Each cannon costs 200 denarii: you start with the six of the starter galley, you buy another by putting it on a ship that has none free to place, and you keep what you buy (move cannons between ships for free). At a ship's cannon limit, clicking an empty cannon position moves the nearest cannon there.
- **The Via Domus and robberies (`robbery.js`, online):** a street of houses east of the Forum (in Places). Everyone who is online has a house on it, with their name over the door and the look of their house tier (garden, wings, tower). Walk up to someone else's door and press `F` to break in: you take 100 denarii, pick a getaway vehicle (or run on foot) and must get clear before the owner catches you. The owner is told at once, sees a red alert with the direction and distance to you, and wins if they get within 4 m of you: you lose 100 denarii to them. If you get 230 m away or 80 seconds pass, you keep 100 of theirs. The owner must be online and in Rome; you rest 90 s after a robbery and cannot rob the same house for 5 minutes. The city guards also run after every thief (they set off together with the owner and get a little faster the longer the chase goes on (5.5 m/s at first, 8 m/s at most)), and four of the houses on the street belong to people who are always home (Gaius, Livia, Marcus and Julia), so you can rob on your own: only the guards chase you then. There is no place to reach: you just have to get away, and the alert tells you which way the chaser is. The server decides who is caught.
- **The job board (market squares in Rome and Delhi):** press `F` at the board at the east end of the market for four more jobs, with local names in Delhi: **Baker** / halwai (hold `Space` to keep the oven in the green), **Scribe** / munshi (type the letters shown: `A S D F J K L`), **Stable hand** / mahout's helper (repeat the order the animals light up, keys `1`-`4`) and **Messenger** / harkara (carry a message to a landmark across the city before time runs out; the directions arrow shows the way). They pay in denarii by your rank, like the Colosseum jobs, and festival days pay a quarter more.
- **The harbour (`harbour.js`):** a big stone basin at Portus (about 155 m by 100 m), south-west of the Forum (in Places as *The Harbour*). Stand at the gate and press `F` to choose which of your saved ships to sail (with a picture, hull, speed, cannons and how much cargo it holds) and sail it (`W` `S` `A` `D`, cannons `Q` `E` `Space`, `Esc` ties up). **Fishing:** slow down over a ring of ripples and press `F` to cast, then `F` again when the float dips ("A bite!"). **Trading:** sail up to a pier and press `F` to sell your fish (14 each) and cargo and load more: grain from Ostia wharf (14) sells at Portus quay (23), and wine from Portus quay (18) sells at Ostia wharf (29). How much you can carry depends on your ship. **Pirates** come for ships that carry fish or cargo: sink one for a 35 denarii bounty; if they sink you, you lose your hold and 40 denarii.
- **More vehicles:** at the animals and rides stall, for the modern cities: a **bicycle** (60), **motorcycle** (350), **taxi** (450), **SUV** (700), **delivery truck** (600) and **bus** (900), besides the scooter, city car, sports car and rickshaw; and for the ancient cities a horse-drawn **chariot** (280), besides the horse, camel, donkey, elephant and bullock cart. Every one is taken out and driven the same way (`X` to get in, `W` `S` `A` `D`), and shows in your garage.
- **More animals to buy:** companions at the animals and rides stall: a **cat** (90), **parrot** (130), **monkey** (140), **peacock** (160), **owl** (170) and **lion cub** (260), besides the hound and the eagle. The ground ones trot after you; the parrot, owl and eagle circle above you. Other players see them too. And three more animals to ride in the ancient cities: a **horse** (300, 65 km/h), a **camel** (220, 40 km/h, high up) and a **donkey** (70, 29 km/h), besides the elephant and the bullock cart. Take them out and climb on with `X` like a car.
- **More boats:** twelve new boats in the shipyard (and the harbour and the naval battles): a **rowboat**, a **jon boat**, a **jet ski**, a **bowrider**, a **center console**, a **pontoon boat**, a **sailboat (monohull)**, a **catamaran**, a **cabin cruiser**, a **houseboat**, a **trawler** and a **yacht**, each with its own size, hull, speed, turning and number of cannons (from 1 to 12). The jet ski is the fastest and the houseboat the slowest and toughest.
- **Admin: where everyone is:** the admin panel's list of players online now shows each player's city, the nearest sight ("at The Colosseum", "near Lahori Gate"), what they are doing (walking, riding, in a duel, waiting for a race, robbing a house...) and their coordinates. `/who` in the chat gives the same in short.
- **Pictures in the chat:** press the 📷 button next to the chat box (or paste a picture into it) to send a picture to everyone. The browser shrinks it to a tiny thumbnail (at most 96 pixels across) so it fits through the game server; anything you type in the box goes with it as a caption. Click a picture in the chat to see it bigger. You need to be logged in, one picture every 8 seconds, and people who join later do not get old pictures.
- **Sing a song:** type `/sing` in the chat for a surprise song, or `/sing row`, `/sing twinkle`, `/sing grace`, `/sing auldlangsyne`, `/sing jingle`, `/sing legion`, `/sing gladiator`, `/sing delhi`, and 20 more: `london`, `lamb`, `macdonald`, `yankee`, `susanna`, `greensleeves`, `frere`, `mountain`, `oldman`, `spider`, `silent`, `deck`, `shanty`, `chariot`, `saturnalia`, `diwali`, `holi`, `market`, `friends` and `rain`. `/songs` lists them all. Each line appears in the chat for everyone, and over the singer's head if you can see them. One song at a time, with a short rest between.
- **Cancún and Chichén Itzá (`cancun.js`, a third city):** press `B` (or the ✈ Travel button) and choose Cancún from the list. It is the Yucatán in Mexico, in two eras (`K`): the great **Maya city of Chichén Itzá around the year 1000** with the Caribbean coast (Tulum's walled port, thatched huts and canoes), and **Cancún today**, with the ruins' visitor centre, Kukulcán Boulevard and the Hotel Zone with its pyramid-shaped hotel, beach umbrellas and boats. At Chichén Itzá: **El Castillo**, the nine-terraced pyramid with 91 steps up each of its four sides (you can climb all four, to the temple at the top), the **Great Ball Court** with its stone rings, the **Temple of the Warriors** with its stairway and the **Group of the Thousand Columns**, **El Caracol** the round observatory (climb its stairs), the **Sacred Cenote** at the end of the white road (the sacbé), the **Platform of Venus** and the **Tzompantli**. 13 sights to discover. You have a casita (a house with a door and a hammock) to sleep in, a market with its five stalls (food: Maya tamales, pozol, grilled fish and cacao in the old days; tacos al pastor, ceviche, elote and horchata today; plus three healers' cures), tropical weather with a hurricane season, and two festivals: the **Equinox of Kukulcán** (a zigzag shadow serpent slides down El Castillo's north stairway) and **Día de Muertos** (marigold garlands and candles with `L`).
- **Snorkelling and the cenote:** wade into the Caribbean (a prompt on the beach tells you to walk east into the sea; today, a mask, snorkel and fins cost 15 denarii, charged the first time you press `F` in the water, or hire them at the hut on the beach) or into **Cenote Azul**, an open sinkhole in the jungle with a dock, and press `F` in the water to put your face in it, and `Esc` (or `F` again) to come back up. While you snorkel the camera is always first person (you see through your mask); your usual camera comes back when you surface. You float at the surface and look down at the coral reef, sea fans and grass, a wreck, and schools of fish. Swim close to the twelve creatures (parrotfish, queen angelfish, clownfish, blue tang, sergeant major, lionfish, moorish idol, Nassau grouper, barracuda, green sea turtle, spotted eagle ray and the little cenote tetra), with over three hundred fish in schools over the reef, to add each one to your list (the list of creatures shows on the left of the screen while you snorkel: the ones you have seen by name and the rest as ???): 20 denarii for each the first time, and 300 more for seeing all twelve, after which the list is wiped clean and you can start a new round (the list on the screen says so). Swimming is about as fast as walking (a little quicker at the surface, a little slower with your face in the water); hold `Shift` to swim faster.
- **Doors on houses and no weather indoors:** your houses in Rome and Delhi and the casita in Cancún have real wooden doors that swing open as you walk up and close behind you. Under a roof (or with your face in the water) no rain, snow, hail, leaves or wind blow in, and the sound of the storm is muffled. The little specks of snow, hail and leaves are soft round dots that stay low, not hard squares floating against the sky.
- **More houses to get:** the Market's builder now sells two more levels of house in Rome and in Old Delhi. In Rome: the **Patrician palace** (2,500 denarii, heals 4 times faster, rent 90 a morning: a great hall behind the villa with a columned portico, a great door, four corner towers and a dome) and the **Imperial palace** (6,000, heals 5 times faster, rent 200: golden roofs and domes, a triumphal gate at the road and statues along the path). In Delhi: the **Shahi palace** (2,500: a pillared red sandstone Diwan-i-Aam in the front courtyard with a throne alcove and chhatris) and the **Imperial Qila** (6,000: four minarets at the corners of the plot and golden domes on the hall). Other players see your house's level on the Via Domus (the palaces add towers, golden caps and a gilded gate there).
- **Sail with a friend:** in the **Friends** window, press **⚓ Sail** next to a friend who is online (both of you need to be in Rome). They get an invitation in their Friends window; when they press **Sail!** you both start a **crew battle**: your two ships sail together, with your own saved designs, against three practice ships in the flooded arena. Your cannonballs and rams never hurt your crewmate. Sink every practice ship to win (70 denarii each, and it counts towards your rank); if both of you sink, you lose. Invitations run out after 90 seconds.
- **A plane you can fly (modern cities):** the animals and rides stall sells a **light plane** (3,500 denarii) in Modern Rome, Modern Delhi and Modern Cancún. Take it out (it appears right where you stand, and you are in it at once; if something is in the way it is moved to the nearest clear spot) on a long straight street or a big square, and: `W` more engine power, `S` less power (on the ground it brakes, and then backs the plane up slowly), `A` `D` turn (it banks), `Space` climb, `Shift` dive, `C` **pilot view** (you sit in the cockpit: an instrument panel with a working speed dial, altimeter and compass, a throttle lever, a control wheel that turns as you steer and the window posts; the horizon tilts as the plane banks; press `C` again for the chase view). Above about 100 km/h hold `Space` to take off; below about 95 km/h in the air it sinks. Land gently (not too fast or too steeply, or you crash and lose health) and press `X` to get out. Fly to the **edge of a city** (high up) and press `F` to **fly on to the next city**: Rome → Delhi → Cancún → Rome. Buildings can be crashed into at low height, and police cars cannot stop a plane in the air.
- **Police cars (modern cities, `police.js`):** in Modern Rome, Modern Delhi and Cancún today, knocking people over, running them down in a car or robbing a house raises your wanted level, and from the first star the **police send cars**: white and blue, with flashing red and blue lights and sirens. They come from behind and from ahead, drive as fast as you do (a little faster the more wanted you are), swerve round buildings, try to box you in and ram you if you do not stop, so **you cannot simply drive away**. If one stays right beside you, or two are close while you are slow, you are pulled over and arrested (a fine, and you are let out at the Forum, the Lahori Gate or Chichén Itzá's plaza). More stars mean more cars (up to four). Hide until the stars fade to lose them. The ancient cities still have only the city guards on foot.
- **Chasing a thief:** when someone robs your house you get a **Give chase** window (run on foot, or take one of your own cars, carts or animals), a tall **red light** over the thief that you can see from far away, a **gold arrow** at the top of the screen that points the way, and you run **20% faster** while the chase lasts. Get within 4 m of them before the clock runs out and you win back your 100 denarii.
- **Jails (`jail.js`):** the **Carcer** (the Mamertine Prison, a sight at the foot of the Capitoline hill by the Forum) in Rome and the **Kotwali** (the lock-up on Chandni Chowk) in Old Delhi. Get caught stealing (a robbery that ends with you caught, or the guards catching you while you are badly wanted) and you are locked in a barred cell for 60 seconds with a 50 denarii fine; the time left shows on the screen, and you can bribe the guard (150 denarii) to open the door at once. You cannot travel or teleport while you are locked up.
- **City guards (`guards.js`):** soldiers of the city watch (police in the modern cities) walk their rounds and stand guard at the gates, the Forum, the market and the main sights in Rome and in Old Delhi (about 20 in Rome, 17 in Delhi). They wear a bright cloak and carry a lantern that glows after dark, and they show as small red dots on the minimap and the map. Commit a crime and your **wanted level** (shown as stars at the top) goes up: robbing a house sends it to the top at once, and each person you knock over adds to it. When it is high enough, any guard who sees you (within about 45 m) shouts and runs after you, and calls the others nearby. They run faster the more wanted you are; a fast vehicle can outrun them. Get out of their sight for a few seconds and the level falls. If a guard catches you, you pay a fine of 100 denarii (or all you have), spend a moment in the cells and are let out at the guardhouse by the Forum or the Lahori Gate. In the middle of a robbery, being caught by the guards ends the robbery as caught.
- **Pay for shifts:** every finished shift pays at least 30% of the job's full pay, even if you play it badly, up to the full pay (plus 10 for a perfect one) times your rank and any festival bonus. The screen shows about how much you have earned so far during the shift. Leaving a shift early pays nothing.
- **The Jobs button:** the 💼 Jobs button at the top lists every job in the game, grouped by where you find it (the hypogeum, the Circus Office and the market job boards in Rome and Delhi), with what each one is, what it pays at your rank, and *Show me the way* and *Take me there* buttons for the places in your city.
- **Show password:** the password boxes (log in, sign up and choosing a new password) have an eye button 👁 at the right: press it to see what you typed, and press it again (🙈) to hide it. The box always starts hidden when you open the account window.
- **Armourer (weapons for the arena):** a stall at the south edge of the gladiator arena in Rome sells weapons: a **sica** (dagger, 60: quick and light), a **hasta** (spear, 130: long reach), a **fuscina** (trident, 200), a **securis** (battle axe, 220: hits very hard but is slow and tiring) and a **champion's greatsword** (320). Walk up and press `F`, buy one and press **Equip**. You fight with the gladius unless you equip another. Each weapon changes your damage, reach, swing speed and stamina cost, in the arena against the bots and in duels and team battles against real people (who see the weapon in your hand).
- **Shove:** press `R` in the streets to shove the person in front of you so they fall over: a citizen, or another real player walking in the same city (they are pushed back two steps and drop to the ground for a moment, with no damage, and the game server checks you really are that close; nobody can be shoved twice within a second and a half). A prompt at the bottom says "R: shove <name>" when someone is in front of you, and the **🤜 Shove** button in the menu does the same. Running into citizens knocks them over too. The guards notice, and knock over too many and you face the trial by combat.
- **Festival lamps for everyone:** the diyas and sky lanterns you set down at Diwali and Saturnalia are seen by every other player in the same city (and a lamp stays lit for half an hour for people who arrive later).
- **Menu button:** the row of buttons on the right is folded away behind a **Menu** button (a fireball with a ☰ in the middle) (Directions is in the menu too). Press it to show all the buttons and press it again to hide them. The game remembers your choice, and the fireball on the Menu button burns blue when a daily gift or a friend's message is waiting.
- **Rank:** every game you play against real people counts towards your rank, and your rating moves with the result (Elo, starting at 1000: Tiro, Gladiator, Veteran, Champion, Legend of the Arena). **Duels** are matched with someone of a similar rating (the longer you wait, the wider the search). **Races** and **naval battles** score each place against the others, so first place gains the most and last place loses the most. **Team battles** against the bots need at least two real people: a win is +8 and a defeat is -6. Practice bots never count, leaving a game counts as a loss, and meeting the same people more than three times in ten minutes changes nothing. A duel win also pays 40 denarii. The **leaderboard** in Play online shows the top ten, and your tier icon and rating sit in a small badge beside your name on the main screen. You need an account to keep a rank.
- **Friends:** the **Friends** button lists your friends (🟢 online), shows requests, and lets you add someone by name or from the list of players online. Both people must agree, and then you can **chat** privately: a message sent while your friend is away waits for them. You need an account, and the friend must be online to receive a request.
- **Daily gifts:** the **Daily gift** button glows when today's gift is ready. Seven gifts in a row (denarii, a hot meal, a healing salve, and a feast on day 7), and every week after that adds a little more; miss a day and the streak starts again.

Rome has eleven (by the Colosseum, the Via Sacra, the Forum, the Capitoline, the Pantheon, Trajan's Forum, the Circus Maximus, the harbour, the Forum Boarium, the Theatre of Pompey and the Via Domus) and Old Delhi eight (the Lahori Gate, the Red Fort, Chandni Chowk, Fatehpuri Masjid, the Jama Masjid and more). Press `F` at one for the same dishes as the market's food stall (in the right city and era, and festival dishes on a festival).
- **Diseases (`disease.js`):** you can fall ill. **Cold** (from standing in rain, snow or hail; a cloak halves the chance), **marsh fever** (out after dark by the harbour in Rome, or in the monsoon in Delhi), **food poisoning** (street food is sometimes bad, more often in summer), **heatstroke** (Delhi's summer midday sun) and **plague** (an outbreak sweeps each city for three days a year: being in a crowd then can infect you). Symptoms: sneezes and coughs over your head, a tinted screen, slower walking, health that drains or heals slowly. While you are ill and away from your house you lose health (more for fever and plague), and at home you do not; an illness never kills you (it leaves you at 5 health). Illness is uncommon: it takes a good while out in the wet (or the night air, or the midday sun) before you can catch anything, the chances are small, and for five minutes after you get better you cannot catch the common ones again. Being hungry makes you catch things more easily, sleeping at home and resting speed up recovery, and shade or a cool drink cure heatstroke. The healer at the market sells medicines (a herbal drink for 10, a tonic for 40, and the strong theriac / rasayana for 130, which cures even plague). Your illnesses are shown under your stats and saved with your game.
- **Plays at the Theatre of Pompey (`theatre.js`):** sit in the seats (or just stand in the theatre) and press `V` to watch a programme of five acts on the stage: a prologue with the chorus, a comedy (Plautus' *The Pot of Gold*, with a chase), a tragedy (Seneca's *Medea*), music and dance (a pantomime with an aulos player) and a parade for Pompey's triumph. Masked actors say their lines over their heads and captions explain the history. `C` is a cinematic camera, `N` skips to the next act, `1`-`5` picks one, `V` or `Esc` stops. Ancient Rome only.
- **Two new sights in Rome:** the **Temple of Hercules Victor** in the Forum Boarium (a round marble temple with twenty columns; in Modern Rome it still stands, minus its roof) and the **Theatre of Pompey** in the Campus Martius (walk in round either end of the seats, where the signs are; *Take me there* puts you in the orchestra) (a half-round of seats, a stage building, the temple of Venus Victrix on top and the garden portico behind where Caesar was killed; in Modern Rome only its history remains). There are now 20 sights to discover in Rome (with the Carcer).
- **More Rome jobs:** on the market job board in Ancient Rome there are four more: **water carrier** (lean with `A`/`D` to keep the buckets balanced), **merchant's haggler** (press `Space` to sell at the highest price a customer will pay), **armourer's hand** (strike with `Space` when the iron glows orange) and **night watchman** (press the arrow that shows which way the thief goes). And at the **Circus Office**, a hut by the starting gates of the Circus Maximus (in Places), four racetrack jobs: **horse trainer** (repeat the order), **track raker** (`A`/`D`), **starting-gate keeper** (`Space` in the green) and **wheelwright** (`Space` on orange iron).
- **Festivals (`festival.js`):** the year has 28 days and the cities celebrate. Rome: *Floralia* (days 4-6, flower garlands) and *Saturnalia* (days 25-28, lanterns along the Via Sacra). Delhi: *Diwali* (days 9-12: strings of lamps along Chandni Chowk, fireworks in the night sky, mithai) and *Holi* (days 17-18: clouds of coloured powder and flags). A badge at the top shows the festival. While it lasts, food is a quarter cheaper, jobs pay a quarter more, the food stall sells festival dishes, people shout greetings and the music is brighter.
- **Music (`music.js`):** background music made in the browser, different for each city and era (a Dorian lyre and flute in ancient Rome, a raga with a tanpura and tabla in Mughal Delhi, and so on). It slows in rain and at night, and has its own Music on/off button.
- **Chat:** stays open after you send a message; Esc or Enter on an empty line closes it.
- **Tax and reputation:** every 30 days you owe the tax collector (20 denarii, more for each house upgrade you own and a little for being rich). A line under your stats counts the days down, you get notices 5 days and 1 day before, and the 💰 Tax button pays it. Miss it and people hate you: they shout things like "Tax dodger!" over their heads as you pass, the Market charges 25% more, and after 15 days overdue the collectors seize your savings. People forgive you over a few days after you pay. **Ramming people:** when you knock someone over they shout back in a speech bubble ("Watch where you're going!", "Arre! Dekh ke chalo!", "I'll call the police!" depending on the place and time, and angrier if you were running).
- **Directions:** click the big yellow **Directions** button (or press `G`) and pick a place. You get a walking route around the buildings, drawn on the minimap and map, gold markers on the ground, a light beam at the destination and a card with the distance, walking time and turn-by-turn text. `G` also opens the panel; there is a "nearest place I haven't seen" shortcut. Click a pin on the map for directions too
- **Sound:** everything is synthesised, no audio files. Places make sound you can hear from a distance (the crowds of the Colosseum and the Circus, market chatter in the Forum, birds and wind on the hills, water in the naval battle). It gets louder as you approach, pans to the side it comes from and is muffled when it is behind you. Footsteps change with the ground, and there are effects for swords, roars, cannons, hooves and more. `O` turns sound on and off
- **Crashes:** run into a building, a wall or a column and you crash: the screen flashes and shakes, you stagger, you are knocked back a step and you lose some health (more if you were running). Run into a citizen and you knock them over. The Circus Maximus has a podium wall between the track and the seats (use the stair entrances to climb up) and a tall wall behind the stands, so running into them hurts too. Walking into things is only a gentle bump. **Days and nights:** a day lasts 20 minutes (50 seconds per hour) with a real sunrise, sunset, moon and stars; the clock is at the top. After dark, go to Your House (near the Colosseum road, in Places and Directions), step inside and press Z to sleep: you wake at dawn fully healed and the horses gain a little. Stay awake until 4 am and you collapse, wake in your bed and lose 15 denarii. T skips ahead in the day, but not through the night (it stops at 4 am and you collapse); from 10:30 pm the clock warns you that you are sleepy. **Touch controls:** on a phone or tablet, a joystick (bottom-left) drives movement, dragging anywhere else looks around, and Jump/Run buttons appear bottom-right. Fights, races and naval battles get their own on-screen Attack/Block, Whip/Lane-hop/Cruise, or Fire buttons. Desktop keyboard and mouse are unaffected. **Character and class:** the first time you enter Rome you choose a name, a class (Gladiator, Charioteer, Venator or Merchant, each with bonuses and a weakness) and your look (skin, hair, toga, sash). Press Y to change it later. **Ancient Rome / Modern Rome:** press K (or the toolbar button) to switch the whole city between 128 AD and today. Modern Rome is shown as it looks now: the Colosseum keeps its tall wall on the north side and has collapsed to a low stub on the south; temple and basilica roofs are gone and many columns are broken or missing (and no longer block your way); the paving is cracked, with missing slabs and weeds, and the Colosseum arena floor is gone, showing the chambers beneath; the Circus Maximus is a grass park; the Colossus is gone; apartment blocks have rows of windows; asphalt streets with lane markings run between them, with parked cars and street lamps you can crash into (the lamps glow at night); and there is moving traffic: cars drive the streets, brake for each other and for you, honk, and hit you if you step in front of one at speed. Traffic hum replaces the crowd noise. Ruined parts of the Colosseum and columns are real in the physics too: only things that stand today still block you. The Colosseum, Forum and other landmarks weather into ruins (or, for the Pantheon and a few others, stay much the same), the Circus Maximus becomes a grass park, the Colossus disappears (it was melted down centuries ago), apartment blocks and citizens switch to a modern look, and the Colosseum/Circus games only work in Ancient Rome. Landmark cards show a green 'Today'' note in Modern Rome. **Team battle (play online):** 🌐 Play online > *Team up: beat 5 bots together*. ## Security

The game is a static site: no server code, no accounts, no database and no network requests, so there is nothing to break into on the server side. What is protected:

- **Content-Security-Policy** (in the page, and as real headers via `_headers` / `vercel.json`): only the game's own scripts and the one pinned 3D library may run. Injected inline scripts, inline event handlers and scripts from other sites are blocked.
- **Subresource Integrity** on the Three.js CDN script: if the CDN copy ever differs from the exact file checked in `index.html`, the browser refuses to run it.
- Other headers: no framing (clickjacking), no MIME sniffing, no referrer, no camera / microphone / location.
- Saved progress in the browser is validated on load (unknown landmarks dropped, health and money clamped).
- No secrets are stored in the repo.

**What cannot be prevented:** because the game runs in the player's own browser, a determined player can change their own health, money or position with the browser developer tools. That only affects their copy. If you ever add a shared leaderboard or prizes, scores must be checked on a server.

**Keep your accounts safe:** turn on two-factor authentication on GitHub and on the host, use a GitHub token limited to this one repo with a short expiry, and revoke tokens you no longer use.
