Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

An account is needed to play

You log in, or create an account, before you enter the game. Your progress is also kept in your own browser (local storage) on your device, and is copied to your account so that it follows you to other devices.

There is also a demo that you can try without an account. It does not save anything to an account and it has no other players and no chat: progress stays only in your own browser. Like every visit, the game server still notes that the page was opened and keeps the short list of what you do (see "What you do each visit" and "Visits and security" below).

Your account

We collect: your email address and a password (handled by our account provider, Supabase, which stores only a secure hash of the password), and your saved game: your character (name, class, look), the landmarks you have discovered, your health, horses and play money (called aspcgua, the game's pretend coin), and the in-game time. We use this only to log you in and to save and restore your game.

Chat

Chat messages go through the game server to the other players who are online. The server keeps the last few messages in memory only (so newcomers see them), and the last 300 public messages, with the name of who sent them, for the game's admins; it does not store them anywhere else, and a restart of the server empties them. Whispers go only to the person you write to. Chat can also carry a small picture (a tiny thumbnail): it is kept in memory with the message, like the text. Email addresses, links, phone numbers and @names are replaced by "[hidden]" before a message is sent on, and swearing is masked. A player can report a message with the flag next to it (or with /report): the report sends the sender's name and the text of that message to the admins, who can mute or remove people who misbehave. You can turn pictures in chat off in the menu ("Chat pictures"): you will then neither see nor send them.

What you do each visit

To help the admins spot cheating and see how the game is used, the game keeps a short list of what you do each time you come on: for example that you opened the game, went to Delhi, bought a plane, finished a job, started a naval battle, or died, with the time of each. It never includes chat messages or anything you type. It is kept in the game server's memory only (the latest few visits of each visitor), is shown only to the admins next to the random visitor id, your character name and, if you are logged in, your account, and is gone when the server restarts. It is also collected in the demo.

Invite links

The Friends window has an invite link that contains your public friend id (a scrambled code, not your email or name). If a new player opens it and plays for a few minutes, the game server saves, with the rest of the game's shared data, that their friend id was invited by yours, so that both of you get the in-game bonus once. The bonus is made-up game money only. Nobody is told who you are except by the name you chose for your character.

Seeing other players

While you walk around, the game server learns where you are standing (a position in the city, no more) and shows your character name, class and look to other players near you in the same city. This position is not stored. There is no text or voice chat.

Playing online

When you play real people, the game server receives your character name, class, look and your movements or race progress during the match, and shows the name, class and look to your opponent. This is not stored after the match. There is no text or voice chat.

Visits and security

When you open the site, and when you play online, the game server notes the time, a random visitor id that your browser made up (it is not linked to who you are), the kind of browser and device, your internet address (the full address is shown only to the game's admins, never to other players), and your character name or account email if you have one. It also notes suspicious behaviour, such as sending messages far too fast, moving impossibly fast, trying admin commands or choosing an offensive name. This is kept in the server's memory only (about the latest few thousand visits), is lost when the server restarts, and only the game's admins can see it. We use it to keep the game safe and to remove people who misbehave.

Who we share data with

Only the services that run the game for us: Supabase (accounts and saves) and our hosting providers. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Classes (for teachers and schools)

Class mode is for a class taught by a teacher, who gives the students a link or a class code. There is no account and no login. Progress (the character, the play money, what was found) is kept only in that browser tab and is gone when the tab is closed. Class mode has no other players and no chat, and the game does not send the page opening or the list of what you do to our server, and does not check character names (so teachers may want students to use made-up names).

The one exception is when the teacher turns on "Let me watch what each student does" on the teacher page. Then the student's screen says "your teacher can see your progress", and every few seconds the student's browser sends the game server: the name the student chose for their character, the city and era, the play money, the sights found, a few counts (shops visited, errands, goods made, achievements), the progress of a scavenger hunt, and short lines about what they did (for example "found the Pantheon"). It never includes chat or anything typed, and no internet address is stored with it (the server only counts calls per address for a minute, to stop floods; our hosting provider may keep ordinary request logs, as described under "Who we share data with"). The server keeps it in memory only, for at most ten hours after the last report, and never saves it. The teacher reads it on a dashboard that needs a secret key which only the teacher's browser and the dashboard link have. The teacher or school decides whether to use this feature and is responsible for any permission the school requires from parents or guardians before students use it.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it. Younger students can take part only through class mode, run by their teacher, as described above.

Your choices

When you log in you can choose Remember me on this device (on by default): then your login and your email address are kept in this browser until you log out; if you untick it, the login is kept only for the open browser tab. You can log out at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and saved game by contacting us (below). Clearing your browser's data removes the progress stored on your device.
