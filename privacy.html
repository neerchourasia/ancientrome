Privacy Policy

Last updated: see the date of the latest version of this site.

Playing without an account

You can walk around Rome and play the single-player games without an account. Your progress is then stored only in your own browser (local storage) on your device. We do not receive it.

If you create an account

We collect: your email address and a password (handled by our account provider, Supabase, which stores only a secure hash of the password), and your saved game: your character (name, class, look), the landmarks you have discovered, your health, horses and denarii, and the in-game time. We use this only to log you in and to save and restore your game.

Chat

Chat messages go through the game server to the other players who are online. The server keeps the last few messages in memory only (so newcomers see them) and does not store them anywhere else. Whispers go only to the person you write to. Admins can mute or remove people who misbehave.

Seeing other players

While you walk around, the game server learns where you are standing (a position in the city, no more) and shows your character name, class and look to other players near you in the same city. This position is not stored. There is no text or voice chat.

Playing online

When you play real people, the game server receives your character name, class, look and your movements or race progress during the match, and shows the name, class and look to your opponent. This is not stored after the match. There is no text or voice chat.

Who we share data with

Only the services that run the game for us: Supabase (accounts and saves) and our hosting providers. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Children

Accounts are for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can log out at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and saved game by contacting us (below). Clearing your browser's data removes the progress stored on your device.
