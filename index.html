                            Pachisi

                            You are the green pawns, your rival is red. Each cowrie that lands mouth up counts one; none up counts six, and six up gives you another throw. Land on a rival's pawn (away from the four marked castle squares) to send it home. Both pawns must go round the board and into the middle.

                            Bazaar trader

                            Each bazaar has its own prices, and they change every day. Each trade takes 15 minutes, and walking to another bazaar takes 2 hours and costs 2 denarii. The bazaars open at 6 am and close at 7 pm, and then you sleep and a new day begins (six days in all). The money you start with comes out of your purse and comes back, with your profit, when you finish.

                            Play real people

                            Every game against real people counts towards your rank: duels, races, naval battles and team battles. Win to climb. Practice bots never count, and you need an account to keep a rank.

                              Controls

                              Move↑←↓→ or WASD
                              Time and sleepA day lasts about 20 minutes. After dark, go to Your House (directions with G, it is near the road to the Colosseum), step inside and press Z to sleep until morning. Stay up and at 4 am you collapse in the street (the clock warns you from 10:30 pm). T skips ahead in the day, but it cannot skip the night: without sleep you stay up until 4 am and collapse
                              Walking speed1-9 (1 slow stroll, 4 normal, 9 sprint) · hold Shift to run
                              JumpSpace
                              Look aroundDrag the mouse
                              CameraC changes the camera view: close behind, far behind, side, front, bird's-eye, first person (Shift+C goes back). Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out. You can see yourself in every view except first person
                              Rides (cars, rickshaws, carts, elephants)Cars and cycle rickshaws (modern cities) and bullock carts and elephants (ancient cities) are in the Market. Buy one, take it out, walk up and press X to get in. W gas, S brake/reverse, AD steer, Space handbrake, Esc or X to get out
                              MarketWalk to the market square (Rome: south of the Forum; Old Delhi: the open square before the Lahori Gate) and press F at a stall. The food stall sells hot dishes that heal you, the clothier things to wear, the animals and rides stall companions, cars, carts and elephants, the builder a better house and decorations, and the healer salves, fodder and training
                              Character and classY opens the character screen: pick a name, a class (Gladiator, Charioteer, Venator or Merchant, each with its own bonuses) and your look. You are asked once when you first enter Rome
                              Games of Old DelhiN in Delhi opens the list: a race down Chandni Chowk (rickshaws or elephants; 1-9 speed levels, X cruise, Space boost), defending the Red Fort's gate with a cannon, kushti wrestling, kite fights, a chaat (food) run, pachisi and a bazaar trading game. Esc leaves a game.
                              Travel (Rome / Delhi)B (or the Travel button) takes you between Rome and Old Delhi, Shah Jahan's walled city. K there switches between Mughal Delhi (c. 1650) and Modern Delhi. The fights, races and naval battles stay in Rome.
                              Ancient / Modern RomeK switches the whole city between 128 AD and today: the Colosseum and Forum weather into ruins, the Circus Maximus becomes a park, apartment blocks and citizens turn modern, and the Colossus (long gone) disappears. Only works while walking, not mid-game
                              Touch (phone/tablet)A joystick (bottom-left) moves, dragging anywhere else looks around. Jump/Run buttons appear while walking; fights, races and naval battles get their own on-screen buttons
                              TurnQ left · E right
                              Lock mouseDouble-click the view (press Esc to release)
                              MapM or click the minimap. Click the map to teleport
                              Fight (Colosseum)Stand in the arena, press F. Space or click attacks, hold Shift or right-click blocks
                              Animal hunt (Colosseum)Stand in the arena, press U. Hunt a boar, a leopard, a bear and then a lion with a bow and arrows: hold Space or the mouse to draw, release to shoot (a longer draw is stronger and straighter), Shift to dodge roll, WASD to move
                              ShoveStep up to a citizen and press R to shove them over (running into people knocks them over too). Careful: the guards notice, and knock over too many people and you face a trial by combat. Only in the streets (R starts the race on the Circus track)
                              Lion (Colosseum)Stand in the arena, press L to hunt a lion on its own with the bow (same controls as the animal hunt)
                              Naval battle (Colosseum)Stand in the arena, press N. The arena floods and you command a galley with cannons. WS sail, AD steer, Q or E fire a broadside, Space fires at the nearest enemy
                              Race (Circus Maximus)Stand on the track, press R. W gas, S brake, AD steer (hold Shift for fine steering), QE hop a lane, 1-9 speed level (1 slowest, 9 gallop), Space whip, X cruise control, C camera, hold B to look back
                              WatchPress V in the Colosseum to watch the games: an animal hunt, three myth dramas (Orpheus, Icarus, Hercules) and gladiator matches. N skips to the next act, 1-5 picks one. In the Circus stands, V shows a chariot race. C switches camera
                              Rest and healPress Z to sit and rest (move to stand up). You recover on your own while you are not in a game, but resting is about four times faster, and the horses only recover quickly at the Circus stables. Fights and naval battles need health 35 or more, and races need rested horses and a 20 denarii entry fee. Winning earns denarii; losing costs you
                              DirectionsClick the Directions button (or press G) and pick a place. A route appears on the minimap and map, gold markers lead the way on the ground, a light beam marks the destination, and a card at the top gives distance and turns. You can also click a pin on the M map
                              VoiceA narrator reads captions aloud: the subtitles of the Colosseum shows, the history card of each landmark you reach, the result screens and turn-by-turn directions. Press J or the Voice button to turn it on or off. It uses your browser's built-in voice
                              SoundPress O or the Sound button to turn sound on or off. Places make sound you can hear from a distance, such as the crowds of the Colosseum and Circus, and it pans to the side they are on and is muffled when they are behind you
