Time Machine for teachers

Walk ancient Rome (and Old Delhi, Cancún and Beijing) in 3D, in the browser. Make a link, give it to the class: students open it and play at once.

1. Make your class link

Try it

2. What students get

  • No login. They open the link and make a character (a name and a class).
  • Nothing is saved. Each student starts fresh, and the game keeps nothing on the computer after the tab is closed.
  • No other players, no chat. Class mode is offline: nobody from outside the class can appear.
  • No time limit and no limit on denarii (the game's play money).
  • Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on a laptop. The game needs a keyboard and mouse.

3. Controls

W A S Dwalk (hold Shift to run)
mouse (drag), or Q Elook around
Cchange the camera (first person, behind you, from above)
Kswitch between ancient and modern
Gguide me to the nearest sight I have not seen
Ftalk to a shopkeeper, sit on a stool, press the button at a place
Mthe map

Walk up to a shop door and it opens by itself. Every shop has a keeper to talk to (F), who sells, buys, gives errands and tells you where the nearest sight is.

4. Ideas for a lesson

  • Find the Forum, the Pantheon and the Colosseum. Read the Pantheon's inscription (M·AGRIPPA·L·F·COS·TERTIVM·FECIT) and translate it.
  • With Latin names on, walk into the shops and read what the keepers say. Translate each line, then check it against the English.
  • Press K at the same spot to compare the ancient city and today's.
  • Find the places named in a text the class is reading (the Rostra, the Temple of Vesta, the Circus Maximus).

The Latin in the game is the author's own: please check it before you use it with a class, and tell us what to correct.
